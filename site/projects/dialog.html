01-PROJECT BRIEF

Making SIM purchase simpler, starting with the number.

The SIM-buying journey is one of the platform’s key flows. The goal was to rethink the experience into a logical, easy-to-follow journey — helping customers choose a number, verify their details, select a package, and complete the purchase with minimal friction.

Empathize

Existing User Flow

Verification splits into five outcomes — one exact match, several exact matches, one partial, several partials, or none. The no-match path falls back to search. Everything reconverges at the age check.
